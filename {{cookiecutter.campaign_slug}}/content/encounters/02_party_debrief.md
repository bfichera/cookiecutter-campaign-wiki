# Party Debrief

A quiet roleplay scene after the ambush. Use this to hand out clues and let the
players plan.

!!! note "Clues to reveal"
    - The goblins were paid in **silver stamped with a bridge sigil**.
    - Travelers have been vanishing near the **old bridge** for a month.

## Skill checks

- **Investigation (DC 13):** the bark map matches a real crossing two days east.
- **Insight (DC 15):** the goblins seemed *afraid* of whoever hired them.

There are no combatants in this encounter.
