# Goblin Ambush

!!! quote "Read aloud"
    The forest road narrows. Ahead, two dead horses lie sprawled across the
    path, black-feathered arrows jutting from their flanks. The trees are
    unnaturally still.

## Setup

As the party approaches the fallen horses, **four goblins** spring from the
brush on either side of the road.

!!! danger "Trap"
    The goblins have strung a tripwire across the road just past the horses.
    A creature that moves through the square must succeed on a **DC 12 Dexterity
    saving throw** or fall prone.

## Combatants

{% raw %}{{ statblock('monsters/goblin.yml') }}{% endraw %}

## Tactics

The goblins fight from the underbrush (half cover) and flee toward the ravine
if two or more of them fall.

## Rewards

- A crude map scratched onto bark, pointing toward the old bridge.
- 15 gp in mixed coin.
