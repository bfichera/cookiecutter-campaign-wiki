# The Old Bridge

!!! quote "Read aloud"
    Mist clings to a moss-eaten stone bridge. Beneath it, the ravine falls away
    into darkness. Something large paces on the far bank, its breath fogging the
    cold air.

## Setup

A **dire wolf**, bound by the same patron who hired the goblins, guards the
crossing. It attacks anyone who steps onto the bridge.

## Combatants

{% raw %}{{ statblock('monsters/dire_wolf.yml') }}{% endraw %}

## Terrain

- The bridge is **10 ft wide**; only two Medium creatures can stand abreast.
- The mossy stone is **difficult terrain** when wet.

## Rewards

- Passage toward the next leg of the campaign.
- A collar on the wolf bearing the same **bridge sigil**.
