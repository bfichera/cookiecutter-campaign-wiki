# Kael Ironwood

_Human Fighter (Battle Master), Folk Hero_

Kael grew up mending fences and breaking horses on the frontier until a raid
burned his village. He took up his father's blade and never set it down.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Speaks little, acts fast.
- **Ideal:** The strong protect the weak.
- **Bond:** Still carries a scorched horseshoe from home.
- **Flaw:** Distrusts nobility and anyone who wears a sigil.
