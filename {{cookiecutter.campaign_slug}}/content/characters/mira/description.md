# Mira Vale

_Half-Elf Wizard (Evocation), Sage_

Mira left the academy under a cloud of suspicion after an experiment went wrong.
She travels to prove her theories right where the archmages could not.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Answers questions with more questions.
- **Ideal:** Knowledge is the only true power.
- **Bond:** Owes a debt to the librarian who smuggled her out.
- **Flaw:** Cannot resist an unsolved puzzle, even a dangerous one.
